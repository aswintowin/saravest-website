Ground Zero

Amaravathi is being built once. We're helping you own a piece of it.

Amaravathi is Andhra Pradesh's planned greenfield capital, rising on the banks of the Krishna river between Vijayawada and Guntur — government quarters, a new assembly and secretariat, and civic infrastructure going up around land that was farmland a decade ago. It's also one of the oldest inhabited stretches in the state, home to the ancient Amaravathi Stupa.

That combination — a capital being drawn on a clean sheet, next to some of the most fertile, sought-after ground in the region — is exactly why land here behaves differently from land anywhere else in South India.

Saravest deals exclusively in LPS — Land Pooling Scheme — plots: the return plots APCRDA allotted to farming families who pooled their land to build the capital, now reaching the resale market with clear, government-issued title. We don't handle any other plot category, in-line with our Amaravathi-only focus.

The Investment Case

Why buy land in Amaravathi

A capital city gets built once. Here's what's actually funded, planned, and under construction — not projections, but public record.

01

Permanent capital, by law

In April 2026, President Droupadi Murmu's assent to the AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill made Amaravathi Andhra Pradesh's sole, permanent capital — ending over a decade of uncertainty.

02

A city that's actually zoned

A 217 sq.km. master-planned city designed for 3.5 million residents by 2050, with defined density zones instead of unplanned sprawl.

03

Multilateral-funded infrastructure

The World Bank and Asian Development Bank jointly committed ₹13,500 crore toward roads, flood mitigation, water supply, and utilities.

04

Government complex, under construction

Assembly, High Court, and Secretariat layouts are finalized, and Assembly construction is already underway.

05

Twin-city, riverfront location

Built on the Krishna river between Vijayawada and Guntur — one integrated metropolitan region, not an isolated new town.

06

Title you can verify yourself

Every LPS plot carries APCRDA-issued title, publicly checkable on the government's own plot lookup portal — not a private developer's word.

07

An institutional hub, not just offices

VIT-AP University and SRM University-AP both already run full campuses in the Amaravati region — residential demand isn't waiting on government relocation alone.
